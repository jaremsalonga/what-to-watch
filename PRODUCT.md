# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People deciding what to watch tonight, alone or with friends, usually on a phone on the couch with the lights down. English-speaking, Philippines first (Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+, Viu, iWantTFC are common), but any country TMDB covers.

## Product Purpose

"What to Watch?" is a ticket booth: set a mood and how much time you have, roll the film strip, and it prints a ticket for a movie or series you can actually stream on your services tonight. Success is a decision in under a minute.

## Brand Personality

Playful, cinematic, quick. An old cinema ticket booth at night: lit marquee, projector gate, mint ticket stub. Plain English, short labels, a little wit.

## Anti-references

- Streaming-app grids and endless carousels (the thing we're replacing).
- Generic AI app looks: cream backgrounds, rounded white cards, emoji in every label.
- Red-velvet-and-gold cinema clichés.

## Design Principles

1. The roll is the product. The Roll film button is always one tap away.
2. Moods and minutes first; genres, dates, rating and where to watch live in one Filters sheet.
3. Only suggest what people can actually watch (their services, their country).
4. One main action per ticket.
5. Labels say exactly what happens.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast on the dark theme, readable on small phones, works one-handed. Respect reduced motion (the strip stops quickly instead of rolling).
